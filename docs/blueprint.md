# **App Name**: Starbright Atelier

## Core Features:

- Product Catalog: Browse a curated collection of luxury handbags with high-quality images and essential details.
- Product Detail Pages: View detailed information for each handbag, including multiple angles, material specifications, and sizing.
- Shopping Cart Management: Add, remove, and manage selected handbags before proceeding to checkout.
- Secure Checkout Process: Complete purchases securely with a streamlined payment and shipping information collection process.
- User Authentication: Customers can register accounts to save preferences, track orders, and view purchase history.
- AI-Powered Style Recommender Tool: Receive personalized handbag recommendations based on user input regarding occasion, style preference, and existing wardrobe items.

## Style Guidelines:

- The visual design takes inspiration from luxury and radiance, aiming for a modern, sophisticated aesthetic reminiscent of high-end brands. A light color scheme is chosen to enhance the feeling of spaciousness and exclusivity, with gold and warm accents for a touch of glamour.
- Primary color: A rich, vibrant gold (#E8BA30). This color will be used for key headings, call-to-action buttons, and interactive elements, providing a strong contrast against the light background while embodying luxury.
- Background color: A very subtle, warm off-white (#F4F3F0). This provides a clean, airy canvas that allows the product images and golden accents to truly shine, similar to the minimalist backdrop seen on luxury jewelry sites.
- Accent color: A soft, warm coral-peach (#E8987D). This analogous color provides a complementary warmth, used for smaller details, secondary actions, and visual cues, adding depth without detracting from the primary gold.
- Headline font: 'Playfair' (serif) to convey elegance and a high-end fashion appeal with its geometric structure and contrasting thick/thin strokes. Body font: 'PT Sans' (humanist sans-serif) for clear readability and a modern, approachable feel in product descriptions and general text.
- Use minimalist and refined line icons that align with the brand's luxury aesthetic. Icons should be subtle, sophisticated, and predominantly rendered in gold or dark contrasting tones.
- Adopt a clean, spacious layout with ample white space around product images and content to emphasize exclusivity and craftsmanship. Implement a grid system for product display to ensure consistency and a premium feel, inspired by high-end retail platforms.
- Incorporate subtle and smooth transitions for interactive elements, such as hover effects on product cards and discreet fades on page changes, to create an elegant and responsive user experience.
